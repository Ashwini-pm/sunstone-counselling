# Faculty Assessment — Role & Question Spec

_The roles we hire for, and the exact questions that appear in each station — static (fixed), rotating (bank), and the student doubts for the teaching station. Subject banks are kept generic (not tied to one language/topic)._
_Supersedes `question-bank-4-roles.md`. Owner: Neeraj._

---

## Roles (5, in two families)

**Academic faculty** — teach university programme subjects.
1. **Tech Faculty** — university CS/engineering subjects (programming fundamentals, data structures, OOP, DBMS, OS, networks…).
2. **Management Faculty** — university MBA subjects (marketing, OB, finance, strategy, economics…).

**Placement trainers** — prepare students for placements.
3. **Competitive Coding Trainer** — DSA & problem-solving for coding interviews/contests.
4. **Aptitude Trainer** — quantitative, logical & verbal reasoning.
5. **Comms Trainer** — spoken English, communication & soft skills.

The **9-station structure is identical for all five**; only the role-bank prompts change. Shared banks and the two fixed stations are common to everyone.

## Static questions (identical for every role, every attempt)
**1 · Intro & Teaching Philosophy:** "In about 90 seconds, tell us: (1) who you are, (2) what you teach, (3) one belief that shapes how you teach. Then, why would you be a good fit for students who may be first-generation graduates, from Tier-2/3 backgrounds, and use English as a second language?"

**9 · Reflection:** "In 30 seconds: (1) how do you think you performed today, and (2) one thing you would do differently in a real classroom?"

## Shared banks (one pool, all five roles — pick 1)

**5 · Managing Classroom Challenges**
1. Interrupts repeatedly: "Have you actually done this in the real world, or is it all theory?"
2. Challenges relevance, loudly: "This is useless — when will I ever use this?"
3. Keeps chatting with a neighbour and disturbing others.
4. Challenges you aggressively: "That's wrong, I read something different online."
5. On the phone; when asked, says "I'm listening, just carry on."

**6 · Mentoring**
1. "I'm thinking of dropping out — my family wants me to start earning and I feel guilty studying."
2. "I keep failing and want to give up — I don't think I'm smart enough."
3. "My parents are forcing me into a course/track I don't want."
4. "I'm overwhelmed — placement prep, backlogs and family problems at once."
5. "My friends copy and score higher; I feel stupid for staying honest."

**8 · Integrity Check**
1. A student asks you to raise his assessment/internal scores because his family has placement/industry connections.
2. A student offers a favour in exchange for the upcoming test questions.
3. A colleague asks you to quietly pass/clear a weak student.
4. You find a top student copied work; reporting it could cost their placement.
5. A parent offers you an expensive gift after their child's results improve.

_(For placement trainers, read "internal marks" as "mock/assessment scores or certificates" — same judgment test.)_

## Role banks

Each role has three banks: **Micro-teaching** (topics, each with 3 live student doubts), **Resolving Student Doubts** (misconceptions; two are drawn per attempt), and **Lesson Design** (topics).

### 1 · TECH FACULTY (university CS/engineering)
**Micro-teaching topics + doubts**
- **A `for` loop, with a dry run** — "How does the loop know when to stop?" · "How is a while loop different from a for loop?" · "What happens if the condition never becomes false?"
- **What a function is and why we use it** — "Why not just write the code directly?" · "What's the difference between a parameter and an argument?" · "Can a function call itself?"
- **How an array is stored** — "Why does the index start at 0?" · "Can one array hold different data types?" · "What happens if I access an index that doesn't exist?"
- **A class vs an object (OOP)** — "Isn't a class the same as an object?" · "Why do we need objects at all?" · "Can one class create many objects?"
- **Primary key in a database** — "Can a table have two primary keys?" · "How is a primary key different from a unique key?" · "Can it be text instead of a number?"

**Resolving Student Doubts** — "A longer program is a better program" · "If it runs once without an error, it's correct" · "Comparing two objects with == checks their contents" · "An array grows in size automatically" · "If it compiles, it's bug-free" · "Global variables are easier, so always use them"

**Lesson Design topics** — Introduction to loops · Arrays vs linked lists · Functions & parameters · Database tables & keys · Object-oriented basics (class & object)

### 2 · MANAGEMENT FACULTY (university MBA)
**Micro-teaching topics + doubts**
- **Price Elasticity of Demand (real example)** — "If price rises and people still buy, is demand inelastic?" · "Why are some products affected by price and others not?" · "An example from a brand we use daily?"
- **The 4 Ps of Marketing** — "Aren't Place and Promotion the same?" · "Which P matters most?" · "Where does branding fit?"
- **SWOT analysis** — "Is a weakness the same as a threat?" · "Can something be both a strength and a weakness?" · "How is this useful for a real company?"
- **Maslow's hierarchy of needs** — "Must you finish one level before the next?" · "Where does money fit?" · "Is this actually proven?"
- **Break-even point** — "Is break-even the same as profit?" · "What happens below break-even?" · "Do fixed costs change here?"

**Resolving Student Doubts** — "Marketing is basically selling" · "Profit and revenue are the same" · "A brand is just a logo" · "Leadership and management are the same" · "More advertising always means more sales" · "Fixed costs change with output"

**Lesson Design topics** — The Marketing Funnel · STP (Segmentation-Targeting-Positioning) · The 4 Ps · SWOT analysis · Reading a basic P&L statement

### 3 · COMPETITIVE CODING TRAINER (placement)
**Micro-teaching topics + doubts**
- **Time complexity / Big-O basics** — "Why do we ignore constants?" · "Is O(n log n) always better than O(n²)?" · "How do I find the complexity of nested loops?"
- **Binary search** — "Why must the array be sorted first?" · "How is it faster than checking each element?" · "What if the number isn't there?"
- **Recursion (simple example)** — "How does the function remember where it was?" · "What's a base case and why do we need it?" · "Isn't recursion just slower than a loop?"
- **Hash maps** — "How does it find things so fast?" · "What happens if two keys collide?" · "When do I use a hash map over an array?"
- **Two-pointer technique** — "How do I know when to use two pointers?" · "Does the array need to be sorted?" · "How is it better than a nested loop?"

**Resolving Student Doubts** — "A working solution is a good solution, efficiency doesn't matter" · "Nested loops always mean O(n²)" · "Recursion and iteration can't solve the same problem" · "Binary search works on any array" · "Using more memory always makes code faster" · "If it passes the sample case, it passes all cases"

**Lesson Design topics** — Approaching a problem (brute force → optimise) · Time & space complexity · Arrays & the two-pointer pattern · Recursion fundamentals · Hash maps for interview problems

### 4 · APTITUDE TRAINER (placement)
**Micro-teaching topics + doubts**
- **Percentages (quick mental methods)** — "Is 25% always 1/4?" · "How do I do a percentage increase fast?" · "10% of 200 in my head?"
- **Time, Speed & Distance** — "If I double the speed, does time always halve?" · "How do I handle a stop in between?" · "What's the trick for average speed?"
- **Ratio & Proportion** — "How is a ratio different from a fraction?" · "If the ratio is 2:3, how many parts total?" · "How do I split an amount in a ratio?"
- **Probability basics** — "Can probability be more than 1?" · "What does 'and' vs 'or' mean?" · "Is a coin toss always 50-50?"
- **Profit & Loss** — "Is profit on cost price or selling price?" · "Markup vs margin?" · "How does a discount affect profit?"

**Resolving Student Doubts** — "The average of averages is the overall average" · "A 20% rise then 20% fall returns to the start" · "Permutations and combinations are the same" · "Doubling speed always halves the time" · "Probability can be more than 1" · "Percentage and percentage points are the same"

**Lesson Design topics** — Percentages from scratch · Time & Work · Data Interpretation (tables & graphs) · Number systems basics · Logical reasoning (seating arrangements)

### 5 · COMMS TRAINER (placement)
**Micro-teaching topics + doubts**
- **How to start a presentation** — "What if I go blank at the start?" · "Should I begin with 'Good morning' every time?" · "How long should the opening be?"
- **Structuring a self-introduction (interview)** — "Do I talk about family or only career?" · "How long should it be?" · "What if I have no experience yet?"
- **Subject-verb agreement** — "Why is 'everyone' singular?" · "Is it team 'is' or team 'are'?" · "Does this really matter when speaking?"
- **The STAR method for answers** — "What if I can't think of a real example?" · "Is this only for interviews?" · "How much detail in each part?"
- **Active vs passive voice** — "Is passive voice always wrong?" · "When should I actually use passive?" · "How do I spot passive voice quickly?"

**Resolving Student Doubts** — "Speaking fast means you're fluent" · "Good English means using big words" · "Grammar doesn't matter if people understand you" · "A strong accent is bad English" · "Communication is only speaking, not listening" · "Longer sentences sound more professional"

**Lesson Design topics** — Structuring a 2-minute self-introduction · Email-writing basics · Group-discussion do's & don'ts · Common grammar errors Indian speakers make · Telephone/video-call etiquette

## Tallies
- **Static:** 2 (shared) · **Shared banks:** 3 × 5 = 15 · **Role banks:** 5 roles × (5 micro-teaching topics + 15 doubts + 6 doubt-resolution + 5 lesson-design).
- Per attempt a candidate sees: 2 static + 3 shared (1 each) + 1 micro-teaching topic (with its 3 doubts) + 2 doubt-resolution + 1 lesson-design.
